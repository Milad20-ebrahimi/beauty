# Product Brief

## Product Definition

BeautyOS is a personalization-first beauty commerce product for the Iranian market.
It helps users avoid buying the wrong product by building a Beauty Passport and using it to explain product fit.

The product should not compete on the largest catalog, lowest price, or most aggressive discounts.
Its defensible advantage should be decision support, trust, and user-specific product fit.

## Initial Customer

The initial customer is a Persian-speaking buyer, mostly 18 to 35, who already buys skincare or basic beauty products but is unsure which products fit their skin, concerns, budget, and preferences.

The best first wedge is skincare:

- Sunscreen
- Cleanser
- Moisturizer
- Basic acne/dryness/sensitive-skin routines

## Main Customer Problem

The user is not suffering from lack of options. The user is suffering from too many claims, too many products, weak trust, and fear of buying the wrong item.

## Value Proposition

Do not buy the wrong beauty product. Build your Beauty Passport first, then buy with clear reasons.

## MVP Capabilities

1. Beauty Passport
2. Shopping by Need
3. Personal Match Score
4. Similar-Me Reviews
5. Routine Builder

## First User Journey

1. User lands on the homepage.
2. User sees the promise: avoid confusion and wrong purchases.
3. User starts Beauty Passport.
4. User answers a short, non-medical profile flow.
5. User chooses a need, such as sunscreen or acne-prone skin.
6. System shows a small set of recommended products.
7. Each product has a Match Score and explanation.
8. User reviews experiences from similar profiles.
9. User adds a product or routine to cart.
10. User checks out.
11. After purchase, user submits outcome data that improves future recommendations.

## Revenue Model

Initial:

- Direct product sales
- Margin on curated products
- Routine and bundle sales

Future:

- Transparent sponsored placements
- Brand insights
- Sample campaigns
- Affiliate and creator commerce
- Private label

Sponsored placement must never secretly manipulate Match Score.

## Risks

- Incomplete product data makes recommendations weak.
- Fake or shallow reviews damage trust.
- Overbuilding too many categories slows the MVP.
- Medical claims can create legal and trust problems.
- Low initial capital makes inventory strategy critical.

## What Not To Build Yet

- AR makeup
- Complex AI beauty assistant
- Dupe finder
- Price history
- Creator looks
- Sample box
- Advanced loyalty
- Mobile app
- Full marketplace catalog
- Medical diagnosis
